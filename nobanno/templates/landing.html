What is Nobanno?

The traditional agricultural supply chain is layered with middlemen, shrinking farmers' margins. Nobanno offers a "social-media style" marketplace where farmers create visual posts of their harvests and local buyers discover and purchase them directly in bulk.

🌱

Farmers

Post visual listings of crops with weight & price. Get fair pay for every harvest.

🛒

Buyers

Discover fresh, local goods by location, filter by type, and buy in bulk at fair prices.

⚖️

Transparent Model

Customer pays ৳100 → Admin retains ৳10 → Farmer receives ৳90. No hidden fees.

🚚

Delivery

Orders flow from farm to buyer with deliverymen, releasing farmer payouts on confirmation.

How It Works

From harvest to doorstep — a simple, transparent flow.

1

Discover

Farmers post crops with image, weight & price.

2

Match

Buyers search by location & find nearby goods.

3

Buy

Select quantity, checkout via single bulk order.

4

Ship

Farmer ships; deliveryman handles fulfillment.

5

Confirm

Buyer confirms receipt → farmer payout released.

6

Review

Buyer rates the farmer (out of 5) with optional photos.

Who It's For

Four roles, one connected marketplace.

👨‍🌾 Farmer

Social-style listings, inventory management, order hub, and a wallet tracking earnings & pending payouts.

🛍️ Customer

Geo-search, filters & sorting, partial buying, cart & bulk checkout, order tracking and reviews.

📦 Deliveryman

Claim orders within a radius, update status assigned → picked up → delivered.

🧑‍💼 Admin

Analytics dashboard (GMV, profit, hotspots), user management, order ledger, and payouts.
